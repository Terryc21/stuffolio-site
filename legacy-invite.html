You've received a Legacy Wishes invitation

Someone shared their Legacy Wishes with you through Stuffolio. Legacy Wishes is where a person records who they would like to receive their belongings.

This invitation opens in the Stuffolio app on an iPhone, iPad, or Mac. It can't be opened in a web browser, or on an Android phone or a Windows computer.

How to open it

  1. Install Stuffolio on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  2. Sign in to iCloud on that device, if you aren't already.
  3. On that device, open the invitation link from the message or email you received.

No iPhone, iPad, or Mac?

Ask the person who sent the invitation for a PDF copy. Stuffolio can turn their Legacy Wishes into a PDF they can email or print for you.

Please keep the link private

This invitation is personal. Anyone who has the link can open it, so please don't forward it or post it anywhere. If you think it reached the wrong person, tell the sender: they can stop sharing at any time, which turns the link off.

Questions about Legacy Wishes? See Help & FAQ, or learn more about Stuffolio.
