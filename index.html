Try AI for free, at your own pace

Start with 5 Stuff Scout items, 10 AI Assistant searches, 5 receipt and label scans, and 5 Repair, Keep, or Replace looks. Each allowance is separate, with no time limit.

What happens when I use my free allowance?

After you use a feature's starting allowance, it gets a small free refill on the 1st of each month: 1 Stuff Scout item, 2 AI Assistant searches, 1 receipt or label scan, and 1 Repair, Keep, or Replace look. Unused monthly amounts don't carry over.

Subscribe if you need more. Your core inventory features stay free.

Household sharing is included. Each person's optional AI subscription is separate.

Your inventory. Your choice.

Your inventory stays on your device unless you choose to sync it with your own iCloud account or use an online feature. No account is needed for the offline app.

Optional AI and search features send selected photos or item details to service providers with your permission. Most run when you ask; Price Watch checks automatically for items you choose to watch.
