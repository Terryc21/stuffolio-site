Why choose Stuffolio?

Coffee & Code LLC · Updated September 2026

Found something intriguing at a thrift store? Want to organize what you already own? Start with Stuff Scout’s research or Stuffolio’s home inventory. Discover an item’s value and story, keep what you learn, and care for it — all in one app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Start with a find. Or start with your home.

For thrifters: discover what caught your eye

A curious vase. An unfamiliar maker’s mark. A piece you might bring home. Give Stuff Scout photos and the details you know to research its possible maker, era, and approximate value.

If you decide to keep it, save the research with the item. Your photos and findings become the start of a useful inventory record. You can begin with a single find.

See how Stuff Scout works →

For home inventory users: know what you own

Keep photos, receipts, locations, and warranty details together. Record maintenance and plan what you want to pass along. Core inventory features are free.

When something makes you curious — an inherited bowl, an unfamiliar tool, or a piece you might sell — Stuff Scout adds research to the record you already have.

Explore everyday ownership →

Research becomes a record. The record stays useful. Start with the part you need and use the rest when it helps.

Give Stuff Scout the whole picture

Show the details

Add multiple photos of the same item: the whole piece, its maker’s mark, a label, or a detail that could help identify it.

Add what you know

After the first findings, add a name, a date, or the story you’ve been told. Scout uses that additional context to refine its research.

Keep the discovery

Save the findings with the item in Stuffolio. Keep its value and story close as you enjoy it, care for it, or pass it along.

In the thrift-store aisle, Scout helps you investigate a find. Back home, Stuffolio gives those discoveries a lasting place alongside everything else you own.

AI findings and value estimates can be mistaken. Review the evidence, and use a qualified appraiser when you need a formal valuation.

Try AI research at no cost, with an optional subscription for more. See the free allowances.

Make the record useful every day

Know what’s covered

A three-year warranty might cover labor for one year and parts for three. Track coverage phases and component warranties, with alerts before coverage changes.

Remember what worked

Keep service history, support conversations, and “Would buy again” ratings with the item, ready for your next repair or replacement decision.

Plan what comes next

Keep donation records and use Legacy Wishes to record who you’d like to receive meaningful belongings. The item’s story can help you start that conversation.

Inventory, warranty tracking, and passing-along plans do not require an AI subscription. See all features or explore household sharing.

Privacy is part of the choice

Stuffolio stores inventory locally with optional personal iCloud sync. When you request AI help, submitted photos or text pass through our server to an AI provider. Our policy also describes anonymous usage analytics and their controls. Read the privacy policy.

Under My Roof and Home Contents also describe personal iCloud storage. Their App Store labels declare no data collected by the developer. Nest Egg discloses usage data not linked to identity; Itemtopia discloses identity-linked contact, usage, and diagnostic data. Everspruce discloses tracking data. These are developer disclosures, not independent audits.

When a specialist feature matters

Other apps may suit a specific need: Under My Roof for renovation records and room scanning; Itemtopia for shared work assets and selling pages; Home Contents for a simple one-time purchase; Nest Egg for pantry expiry and language support; Everspruce for boxes and QR labels.
